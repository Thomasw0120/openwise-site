# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 18, 2026*

Openwise: Chess Openings is a chess opening trainer made by Thomas Weissert. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: **we don't collect any.**

## What the app stores

The app saves a few things so it can remember where you left off:

- which openings you've practised and how well you did
- when each opening is next due for review
- the openings you picked, and any names or notes you gave your lines
- your settings (for example, whether explanations are shown)

All of this is stored **only on your phone**. It never leaves your device. We can't see it, and nobody else can either.

## What we don't do

- **No accounts.** You never sign up or log in.
- **No tracking or analytics.** We don't record how you use the app.
- **No ads.**
- **No crash reports** or technical data about your phone are sent to us.
- **No sharing or selling.** We have no data, so there's nothing to share or sell.
- **No internet needed to practice.** The only times the app goes online are described in the next two sections.

## Check my games

If you use Check my games, the app asks Lichess for your public games using the username you type. That request goes straight from your phone to Lichess and is covered by [Lichess's privacy policy](https://lichess.org/privacy). We never see it. Like any website, Lichess sees your phone's internet address when the app asks.

The games are compared with your openings on your phone. The username and the results of the last check stay on your phone only, and you can clear the username at any time.

## Pro subscription

If you subscribe to Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple through your Apple account. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple account. The app only learns from your phone whether Pro is on.

## Deleting your data

To delete everything the app has saved, delete the app from your phone. You can also reset a single opening's progress inside the app.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the app's saved progress may be part of that backup, the same as for other apps. That backup is handled by Apple under your own Apple account, not by us.

The app can also make a backup file of your progress, but only when you tap Export backup, and it goes only where you choose to send or save it. The optional daily reminder is set up on your phone only; nothing is sent to a server.

## Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, we'll update this page and change the date above before the change goes live. If we ever wanted to collect any data, we'd ask you first in the app.
