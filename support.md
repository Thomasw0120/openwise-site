# Openwise: Chess Openings — Help

Questions or a problem? Write to us. A person reads every message.

Contact email: twweissert@outlook.com

## My progress

**Where is my progress saved?** Only on your phone. There is no account, so nothing is saved anywhere else.

**How do I move my progress to a new phone?** In Settings, tap Export backup and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone, open Settings and tap Import backup.

**How do I delete everything?** Delete the app from your phone.

## Practice

**A move in a line looks wrong.** Send us the opening and the move. Every line is checked against real games, but mistakes can slip through, and we fix them quickly.

**The daily reminder doesn't show up.** Open your phone's Settings, find the app, and check that notifications are allowed. Then turn the reminder on again in the app's Settings.

## Pro

**How do I cancel?** Open your phone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Or tap Manage subscription in the app's Settings. Apple handles the subscription, so it can't be cancelled by email.

**I paid, but Pro is off.** In the app's Settings, tap Restore purchases. Make sure the phone is signed in with the Apple account you used to buy.

**Can I get a refund?** Refunds are handled by Apple. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

## Privacy

The app collects nothing about you. If you use Check my games, it asks Lichess for your public games. The full policy is on the [privacy page](privacy).
